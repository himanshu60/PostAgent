How PostAgent works

PostAgent is your AI LinkedIn content studio. It researches what's trending in tech, writes on-brand posts with matching visuals, and publishes them to your own LinkedIn account on a schedule you control.

The 3-step flow

✍️

1 · Generate

Type a topic (or hit Run Cycle) and the agent researches trending developer news, then writes a full LinkedIn post with hashtags and a matching illustration.

🔎

2 · Review

Preview each draft, edit the text, swap the image, or reject it. Nothing goes out without your say-so — you're always in control.

🚀

3 · Publish

Publish now, or schedule for later. At the scheduled time the post goes live on your LinkedIn automatically — image and all.

What you get

🤖

Trend-aware writing

Posts are grounded in real, current developer news (Hacker News, InfoQ, GitHub, dev.to and more) — never generic filler. An LLM keeps only topics relevant to a technical audience.

🎨

Auto visuals

Every post gets a clean, professional illustration generated to match the topic — attached natively when it publishes to LinkedIn.

🗣️

Your brand voice

Add examples of posts you like under History and the agent learns your tone, emoji use, and spacing — so drafts sound like you.

⏰

Set-and-forget scheduling

Schedule posts for any date and time. The agent keeps running and publishes them at the exact moment — even while you're offline.

Good to know

🔒 Where do my posts go?

Only to your LinkedIn account — the one you signed in with. Each user's posts, drafts, and data are completely private to them.

✅ Do I approve every post?

Yes. Drafts sit in Drafts until you approve or schedule them. Nothing is posted automatically without you scheduling it.

🔁 Can I edit a draft?

Absolutely — open any draft's Preview to edit the body, change hashtags, or replace the image before it goes out.

🔗 How is my LinkedIn connected?

Through LinkedIn's official OAuth. You grant permission to post on your behalf; we never see your password, and you can revoke access from LinkedIn anytime.
